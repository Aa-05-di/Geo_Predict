🌍 GeoPredict — Global Pulse Alert System 🌐

GeoPredict is an AI-driven sentinel that scans the global news landscape to detect geopolitical events with the potential to ripple through financial markets. Rather than chasing stock price predictions, GeoPredict acts as your vigilant watchtower, sifting through the noise of world events to deliver timely, actionable alerts that keep you ahead of market-moving developments.

✨ Why GeoPredict?

Imagine a tireless guardian that combs through global news, distills critical insights, and whispers warnings of events that could sway markets—before they make headlines in trading rooms. GeoPredict empowers investors, analysts, and enthusiasts with a sharp, AI-enhanced lens on the world’s pulse.

🎯 Core Features

🌐 Global News Surveillance: Scrapes high-impact articles from trusted sources like BBC, Reuters, and Al Jazeera, focusing on geopolitics, economics, and crises.

🧠 Sentiment Intelligence: Leverages NLTK’s VADER to gauge the emotional weight of news, identifying bullish or bearish undertones.

🤖 AI-Powered Relevance Engine: Uses a fine-tuned LLaMA model to filter events with true market-moving potential, cutting through irrelevant noise.

📬 Instant Email Alerts: Delivers sleek, concise email summaries to subscribers, spotlighting critical events with context and sentiment insights.

🛠 Modular & Scalable: A clean, modular codebase ensures easy expansion to new sources, analytics, or alert channels (e.g., SMS, Telegram).

🔒 Secure & Streamlined: Safeguards credentials with environment variables and prioritizes user-friendly outputs.

🚧 Future Enhancements
